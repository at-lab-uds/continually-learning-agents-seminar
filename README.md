### Prerequisites

The seminar is intended for Master's students and advanced Bachelor's students. A background in machine learning or natural language processing is mandatory. Students should be willing to learn about unfamiliar methods and topics while preparing their presentation.

Students are expected to make a serious effort to understand their assigned paper, including its main claims and conclusions. It is acceptable if technical details outside a student's background remain unclear. This will not affect the presentation grade as long as the student is transparent about what they do not understand. Open questions and points of confusion are useful starting points for class discussion.

When choosing papers, students should consider both their current background and their interest in learning more about the topic.

### Format

Each student gives one presentation during the semester. All students must read the assigned papers before each session.

Further information about the format will be added later.

The final paper for the 7 CP version reports on a small independent project related to the seminar. Further details will be discussed in class.

Study programs may differ in which version of the seminar students can take. As a general rule, Computer Science students take the 7 CP version, while LST students may take either version. Students who are unsure should check with their study program coordinator.
